# Rarity Spread
- The highest and lowest rarities in a completed equipment set may differ by at most one rarity level.
- Blue/Purple is valid; Blue/Gold is invalid.

# Attack Optimization
- Attack optimization consists of optimizing: % Attack and % Physical damage / % Magic Damage (Depends on the class)
- Prioritize the following damage type based on the weapon
    - Sword & Shield: Physical
	- Hammer: Physical
    - Bow: Physical
	- Javelin / Spear: Physical
    - Dagger: Physical
	- Dual Blades: Physical
    - Greatsword: Physical
	- Polearm & Shield / Spear & Shield: Physical
	- Staff: Magic
    - Catalyst: Magic
	- Mace: Magic
- The following weapon affixes should be ONLY be used on the weapons (if the weapon is not here, consider it only if in TARGET or as a dummy/fill gem): 
    - Burst: Greatsword, Polearm & Shield
    - Ranged: Bow, Staff, Javelin, Catalyst
    - Bulwark: Sword & Shield, Polearm & Shield
    - Strife: Sword & Shield, Hammer, Dagger, Dual Blades, Mace, Greatsword, Polearm & Shield, Javelin
